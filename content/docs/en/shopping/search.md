# Search and filter products

Open product search from the storefront or a category page. The search page can show category and price filters, a sort menu, and grid or list view controls.

1. Enter a search term or open a category's product search.
2. Apply the filters that match what you need.
3. Change the sort order if the first results are not useful.
4. Select a product card to review its price, availability, and details.

If no products match, clear or relax the filters and try again. Continue with [choosing a product](/en/docs/shopping/product).
