# Request a bulk purchase

Open **Corporate bulk purchases** to read the storefront's business purchase information. The inquiry form asks for your name, email, company, phone, desired quantity, and product details. You can also add a message. Enter a quantity of at least one, then submit the form.

The page reports whether the inquiry was sent or failed. An inquiry is separate from a checkout order; use [Place an order](/en/docs/shopping/checkout) for products already in your cart.
