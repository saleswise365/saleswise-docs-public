# Help

This section explains where to find answers and how to contact the store about a purchase. Support options and policies can differ between stores, so read the information shown by the store where you placed your order.

## Find an answer or contact support

Start with the [help center and store information](/en/docs/support/help). If you need assistance with an order, [contact support](/en/docs/support/contact) and keep your order number available.

## Understand store policies

Read the store's [returns and refunds information](/en/docs/support/returns) and [other policies](/en/docs/support/policies) before making a request. The live store pages contain the terms that apply to that store.

## Ask about a larger purchase

If your store offers business purchasing, use the [bulk purchase guide](/en/docs/support/bulk-purchases) to find the relevant information and contact path.
