# Create a shopper account

Open **Sign up** from the storefront login page. Enter your first and last name, email address, phone number, and password. Reenter the password, review the linked terms and privacy policy, and accept the agreement before submitting.

The form shows password strength and field errors. Correct any errors before submitting. Once your account is ready, sign in to use your cart, addresses, wish list, and orders.

Already registered? Use [Sign in and manage your account](/en/docs/account/account).
