# Addresses and wish list

Sign in and open **My addresses** to review your saved delivery destinations. Checkout also lets you add an address and select one for the current order. You need at least one address to place an order; the checkout page prevents deleting the last saved address there.

Open **Wish list** from the account area to review products you have saved for later. Availability and price should be checked again on the product page before ordering.

Return to [checkout](/en/docs/shopping/checkout) when your address is ready.
