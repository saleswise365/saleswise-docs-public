# Shopping

Use the storefront to find products, compare their details, and build an order. The shops, categories, products, delivery choices, and payment methods you see depend on the store.

## Find a product

Start by [browsing the storefront](/en/docs/shopping/browse). You can narrow what you see through [categories](/en/docs/shopping/categories), explore [shops](/en/docs/shopping/shops), or [search and filter products](/en/docs/shopping/search).

## Decide what to buy

Open a product to [review its details and choose available options](/en/docs/shopping/product). Then [review your cart](/en/docs/shopping/cart), including quantities and the subtotal shown for each shop.

## Complete the order

Follow the [checkout guide](/en/docs/shopping/checkout) to review delivery, payment, and the final total before confirming. After placing an order, use [your orders](/en/docs/account/orders) to follow its progress.
