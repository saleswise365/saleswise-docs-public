# Explore shops

Open **Shops** to see sellers in the workspace. Each shop card can show its category, rating, and product count.

1. Select a category chip to filter the shops, or choose **All categories** to clear that filter.
2. Use the sort menu to order shops by rating, newest, number of products, or name.
3. Select **Visit** on a shop to open its page.

The shop page may offer product filters and a contact button when the seller has a phone number. Products and contact options vary by shop. Continue with [choosing a product](/en/docs/shopping/product).
