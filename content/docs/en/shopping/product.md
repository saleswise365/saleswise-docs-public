# Choose a product

Open a product from a category, shop, or search result. The product page shows its price and availability, along with **Details**, **Specifications**, and **Reviews** tabs. Related products may appear below.

Review the product information and choose the available options and quantity before using **Add to cart**. If the product is unavailable, choose another item. Your workspace may ask you to sign in before continuing to the cart.

Continue with [reviewing your cart](/en/docs/shopping/cart).
