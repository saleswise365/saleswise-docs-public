# Explore categories

Open **Categories** from the storefront. The page shows main categories and products from their related subcategories. Select a category to open its detail page.

On a category page, choose a subcategory to narrow the catalog. If there are no subcategories or products, return to **Categories** or open the product search for a broader result.

Category availability depends on the workspace. For more ways to narrow products, see [Search and filter products](/en/docs/shopping/search).
