# Browse the storefront

Start on your workspace home page. You can browse **Categories**, visit **Shops**, or search for products.

## Categories

Open **Categories** to see the main groups. Select a category or a subcategory to narrow the products. A category page can also link to a full product search for that category.

## Shops

Open **Shops** to see available sellers. You can filter by category and sort by rating, newest, product count, or name. Select **Visit** on a shop card to see its products.

## Search

Use the product search page to refine results with filters and sorting. Switch between grid and list views when those controls are shown.

Continue with [choosing a product](/en/docs/shopping/product).
