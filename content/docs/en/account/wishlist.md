# Use your wish list

Sign in and open **Wish list** to see saved products. Each card can show the current price, rating, and an action to remove the item.

For a simple product, select **Add to cart**. For a product with variants, select **Choose options** to open its product page first. When every saved product is simple, the page can offer **Add all to cart**.

Removing a product from the wish list does not remove an existing copy from your cart. Check price and availability on the product page before checkout.
