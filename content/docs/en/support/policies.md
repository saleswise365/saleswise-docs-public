# Read store policies

Use the storefront footer to open **Privacy Policy** and **Terms & Conditions**. Read the pages for the tenant where you intend to shop before creating an account or ordering. These pages can differ between stores and can change over time; the live storefront text is the current source for its rules.

For an order issue, see [Returns and refunds](/en/docs/support/returns) or [Contact support](/en/docs/support/contact).
