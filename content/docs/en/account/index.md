# Account and orders

Your shopper account brings together the information you need before and after placing an order. Sign in to review your profile, keep delivery addresses ready, save products, and follow your purchases. The options available can vary by store.

## Get into your account

If you are new to the store, [create an account](/en/docs/account/sign-up). If you already have one, [sign in and manage your account](/en/docs/account/account). You can [reset your password](/en/docs/account/password) from the sign-in flow when needed.

## Prepare for checkout

[Manage your delivery addresses](/en/docs/account/addresses) before ordering. You can also [save products to your wish list](/en/docs/account/wishlist) and return to them later.

## Follow an order

Open [your orders](/en/docs/account/orders) to find a purchase, then [view its details](/en/docs/account/order-details) for its status, items, and total. When the store offers it for an eligible purchase, you can [review an ordered product](/en/docs/account/reviews).
