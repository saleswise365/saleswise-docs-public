# Sign in and manage your account

Open **Log in** to access your cart, checkout, orders, addresses, profile, and wish list. If you are new to this workspace, use **Sign up** where registration is available.

If you cannot remember your password, open **Forgot password** from the login flow. Follow the reset link sent to your account; the reset page needs the token from that link. Do not share the reset link.

After signing in, open **Profile** to review your account information. Use the account navigation to reach [orders](/en/docs/account/orders) or [addresses and wish list](/en/docs/account/addresses-wishlist).
